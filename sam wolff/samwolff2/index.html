What I make

Comics

Long-form books and short strips. Pencilled tight, inked fast, lettered by hand. Hollow Kings ran to 128 pages and nearly finished me.

Drawings

Brush, dip pen, charcoal. Mostly people, mostly indoors, usually about two seconds after something went wrong.

Paint & print

Oil on linen when there’s time, risograph editions when there isn’t. Both end up on the same wall eventually.

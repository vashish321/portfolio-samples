Origin story

Say hi
Panel 1 — 2002

The kid with the margins

Every textbook I owned had a fight scene in the margin. Nobody was surprised when I applied to art school; several people were surprised I finished.

Panel 2 — 2008

Six years of other people's books

I art-directed illustrated books for a small press. I learned how a page breathes, how much white space costs, and that a deadline is a creative constraint whether you like it or not.

Panel 3 — 2014

Back to the desk

I quit, rented a third-floor room above a bakery on Dundas Street West, and started drawing every morning before the ovens cooled. That habit is still the whole method.

Panel 4 — Now

Three books and counting

Hollow Kings (2025), The Long Way Down (2023) and Six Panels for a Missing Hour (2022) — all self-published, all under 300 copies, all signed at the kitchen table.

How it works

No card details, no surprises.

1. Fill the basket

Pick sizes and framing where they apply. Nothing is charged here — the basket just builds your order.

2. Send it over

The basket turns into an email with everything itemised. I reply with a total, postage and a payment link within two working days.

3. It goes out flat

Prints between boards, books wrapped, originals crated if they need it. Tracked, worldwide, at cost.

Do you take commissions?

Two slots a year, usually book covers or editorial. Enquire early — the 2026 slots are booked and 2027 opens in the autumn.

Are the originals available?

Most of them. Anything marked sold or in a private collection is gone, but I can usually tell you what else is in the flat file.

How do the editions ship?

Flat-packed between boards up to 60 cm, rolled in a tube above that. Worldwide, tracked, at cost.

Can I visit the studio?

Thursdays and Fridays by appointment. Bring coffee and I will show you the drawer of things that did not work.

Questions?
Just ask.

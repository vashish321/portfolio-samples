How it works

No two prints alike
01

Draw it badly

Thumbnails on the back of a receipt, at postcard size, until one of them refuses to go away. That’s the one.

02

Ink it fast

Brush and dip pen on cotton rag. One sitting, no undo. The first decision should still be visible in the finished thing.

03

Print it short

Two or three colours at a co-op press in the east end, misregistered by a millimetre. Runs of 25 to 300. Then it’s done.

Studio note

“A drawing is a decision you can still see afterwards.”

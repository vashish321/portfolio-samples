Who’s drawing

Hello, I’m Sam.

Commissions open

I make pictures about people who are holding something in — usually about two seconds after something has gone wrong.

I trained as an illustrator, spent six years art-directing other people’s books, and went back to my own drawing table in 2014. The studio is a third-floor room above a bakery on Dundas Street West. It smells like bread and turpentine and I’ve stopped noticing.

Four things I do

Comics. Three self-published books, all under 300 copies, all hand-lettered. Drawings. Brush and dip pen, finished in one sitting. Paintings. Oil and cold wax, built slowly, scraped back often. Prints. Two- and three-colour risograph editions, pulled at a co-op press where a millimetre of misregistration counts as character.

What I’m up for

Book covers and editorial illustration (two slots a year), anthology invitations, festival tables, library and school talks, and a brush-and-ink workshop that runs twice a year for eight people at a time.

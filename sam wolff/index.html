Sam Wolff — site directions

Five ways to show one practice

Same artist. Five completely different sites.

Each of these is a working three-page site built on the same body of work — 30 pieces across comics, paintings, drawings and risograph editions, plus 3 books with every page readable. What changes is not the paint but the architecture: how you find things, what the site expects you to do, and what it is optimised for. Pick a direction and it becomes the brief.

What all five share

One body of work

The same 30 pieces and 3 books appear in every site, re-coloured to each palette, so the comparison is about structure rather than content.

Self-contained

Fonts are bundled as woff2 and every image is inline SVG generated for the site. No CDN, no analytics, no third-party requests, no cookies.

Built to be used

Skip links, visible focus, keyboard paths through every interaction, labelled form fields, reduced-motion support, and layouts verified from 375px to 1440px.

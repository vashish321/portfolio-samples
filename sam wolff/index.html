What every sample includes

Four real pages

Home, a filterable work catalogue, an about page with exhibition history, and a working contact form that opens the visitor's own email client — no backend needed.

Self-contained

Fonts are bundled as woff2, artwork is inline SVG and every page is static HTML and CSS. Nothing is loaded from a third party, so the sites work anywhere they're hosted.

Accessible & responsive

Skip links, visible focus states, keyboard-navigable lightbox, labelled form fields, reduced-motion support and layouts that hold from 320px to ultrawide.
